About

A community chat that belongs to its members

Most chat apps keep every message on a company's servers and sell the good stuff back to you. CelChat does it the other way around: your identity is a key made on your own device, your communities live on their members' devices, and everything is free.

🔗

Peer to peer

Messages, files, voice and video go directly between devices over WebRTC. The website needs no server of its own: it finds peers through free public brokers, and everything it sends there is encrypted.

🎁

Free, all of it

1080p60 screen sharing, big files, animated avatars, custom emoji everywhere, themes, a soundboard and bots. No tiers, no boosts, no ads.

🛡️

Rules everyone checks

A community is a log of signed actions. Every member's app checks every action against roles and bans, so nobody can fake moderator powers, even with a modified app.

Bots

Build a bot in two minutes. No server, no token.

Open Settings, Bots, pick a template, and it is running. Change the code in the built-in editor, try it in a private test console, then add it to your community.

  • Slash commands with autocomplete and typed arguments
  • Embeds, buttons, files, reactions, roles and moderation
  • Runs sandboxed on your device: no access to your keys, DMs or files, no internet unless you allow it
  • Share it as a .celbot file; others review the code before importing
// a dice bot, start to finish
bot.command('roll', 'Roll a die', ['sides:integer?'],
  async (ctx, { sides = 6 }) => {
    const n = 1 + Math.floor(Math.random() * sides);
    await ctx.reply(`🎲 ${ctx.author.mention} rolled **${n}**`);
  });

bot.button('vote:', async (press) => {
  await press.reply(`${press.user.mention} voted ${press.id.slice(5)}`);
});

How it works

Three steps, no email, no phone number

Create your account

Pick a name and a password. Your identity is a key pair created on your device; the password encrypts it there. Save the recovery key.

Start a community

Create one in a click, add channels and roles, and send your friends an invite link.

Talk directly

Your devices connect to each other. Text, files, voice and video flow between you, not through a company.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier and nothing to unlock. CelChat has no servers to pay for: the website is static, peers talk to each other, and introductions go through free public brokers. The code is open source (MIT).

Where are my messages stored?

On the devices of the people in the conversation. A community's history is copied between its members as they come online. If nobody who has a message is online, newcomers wait until someone is, unless the community runs an archive peer.

Do I need an email or phone number?

No. Your account is a key made on your device. Keep the recovery key or a backup file: there is no company that can reset your password.

Is it private?

DMs are end-to-end encrypted. Calls and files go directly between devices when your networks allow it, otherwise through an encrypted relay. The public brokers only see encrypted messages, hashed topic names, timing and IP addresses. It has not had a professional security audit yet.

Why does Windows warn about the installer?

Code-signing certificates cost money and this is a free project, so the installer is not signed yet. Windows SmartScreen then shows "Windows protected your PC": choose More info, then Run anyway. You can also just use the web app.

Can I run my own server?

You do not need one, but you can: the small hub, archive peers, TURN for strict networks and your own copy of this website are all in the repository, with a Docker setup.

Is it finished?

It is a working alpha: everything listed here works and is covered by automated tests, but expect rough edges. Bug reports on GitHub are welcome.
